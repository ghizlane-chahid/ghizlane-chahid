## 🚀 Featured Project

### ⚖️ Arabic Legal Information Extraction | AI & OCR

**Ministry of Justice, Morocco — 2026**

An AI-powered pipeline designed to extract structured information
from Moroccan judicial documents written in Arabic.

The system combines:

- **OCR** for scanned Arabic document processing.
- **Local LLMs** for extracting legal information.
- **Rule-based validation** for improved reliability.
- **Streamlit** for an interactive demonstration interface.

#### My Contributions

- Developed the project's OCR component.
- Benchmarked **Tesseract, EasyOCR, and PaddleOCR**
  on 10 real judicial documents.
- Achieved **10/10 case-number recognition with EasyOCR**
  during the OCR evaluation, compared with 9/10 using Tesseract.
- Applied document preprocessing and OCR optimization
  to improve Arabic text recognition.
- Integrated OCR into the end-to-end extraction pipeline.

**Technologies:** Python, EasyOCR, Tesseract, PaddleOCR,
NumPy, Streamlit.

🔗 [Explore the Project Repository](https://github.com/Aymane-Khoulali/extraction-juridique-pfa)

*Developed collaboratively with Aymane Khoulali,
who worked on the LLM extraction component.*
